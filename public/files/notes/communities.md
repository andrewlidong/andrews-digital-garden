The rooms I keep coming back to. Come say hi at any of them.

- **[NYSRG](https://notes.ekzhang.com/events/nysrg)** — the NY Systems Reading Group. Weekly; each month we pick a systems topic, read about it, and build things in that area.
- **SWE Tea** — a weekly-ish group that reads software engineering and CS papers together, informally and unrecorded. Ask me for an invite.
- **[Side Project Saturday](https://sideprojectsaturday.com/)** — Saturday mornings, side projects, coffee.
- **[Creative Coding NYC](https://www.instagram.com/creativecodingnyc/)** — art + code. The energy behind things like [[Spelling words with the Subway|Subway Spell]].

*This is my current rotation, not a directory — it changes as I do.*
